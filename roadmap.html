Roadmap

Everything on one page: where you are, the whole timeline with your actual progress, and the pathways between steps.

Master timeline: plan vs actual

The one timeline for everything. Bars are your plan (dashed = gated, so the date moves if launch slips); diamonds are single dates, amber ones are gates. Type your real dates in the table below it and they appear here as a thin bar under the planned one (green = on time, red = late, amber = in progress). The strip at the bottom shows how many workstreams run at once.

Edit dates: planned and actual

Planned dates are editable too (MiniMake is set to Jan - Feb 2027 and MiniMate to Mar - Jun 2027, as you planned). Each product's phases are drawn inside its window in proportion to their effort. These dates are shared live with the team (also editable on the To-do page): connecting.... Checklist ticks and decisions on other pages still save in this browser; use Export on the Build page to back those up.

Pathways and gates, all tracks together

Every track on one map, in time order. Arrows show what must finish before something else can start (dashed = a soft dependency). Amber pills are gates. Under each period you can see how many things you are doing at once. Compare it with the busiest months in the timeline above.
