MiniMe Atlas

MiniMe is a personal, workspace-centered AI operating environment: a chat does not disappear when the answer ends, it becomes a durable workspace that a roster of specialised agents works in. This Atlas is the book that explains it, the map of the code behind it, and the record of how both are kept true.
