Styleguide

The visual language for Atlas 2, built before it touches a real page. Every panel below renders twice, in Dark and in Paper, from the same markup and the same tokens. Nothing on this page uses the old per-page CSS. Sample figures are labelled as samples.

Colour

Hex values and contrast ratios are read from the live tokens, so they cannot drift from the CSS. Text tokens must reach 4.5:1 on the three surfaces; the control border must reach 3:1.

What the colours mean

  • Cyan: data flows, the request path
  • Amber: gates, approvals, invariants
  • Crimson: you are here, brand
  • Green: verified, fresh
  • Violet: relations in the graph

Colour is never the only carrier. Each meaning above also has its own shape, and every badge and callout has a label.

Typography

Sora for interface and headings. STIX Two Text for chapter prose only, with a Serif/Sans reader toggle later. JetBrains Mono for code, paths and numbers in tables.
